The research behind the direction

Evidence-informed recommendations, not a claim that one style is objectively best. Usability still needs narrator validation.

Linear · March 2026 refresh

Consistent headers and calmer supporting chrome. Application: stable action placement, quiet nav, stronger focus on the working content.

Carbon · Data tables

Task-oriented tables and contextual actions. Application: retain dense chapter/review lists; move deep evidence into a legible inspector.

Fluent · Drawers

Keep supporting context usable alongside the task. Application: one stable evidence rail, with a dedicated detail mode at narrower widths.

Atlassian · Typography

A small, consistent text hierarchy. Application: preserve Barlow headings, use readable Plex body copy, reserve mono for time and measurements.

Fluent · Progress

Real stages, meaningful units, and an actionable failure state. Application: polish the existing WorkDialog contract, never invent progress.

W3C · Targets and keyboard access

Dense does not mean hard to operate. Application: sufficiently sized controls, visible focus, and no sticky panel hiding the active control.
