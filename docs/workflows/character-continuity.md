# Workflow: Character Continuity Review

## Goal

Help a narrator notice possible variation from their own approved character references across a book.

## Flow

1. Use Manuscript Guide to review characters, aliases, and dialogue cues.
2. Mark representative REAPER regions for a character and explicitly approve them as references.
3. Analyze later candidate dialogue against the approved reference set.
4. Review flagged differences with surrounding manuscript context and A/B audition controls.
5. Accept, dismiss, or annotate the finding; update the approved reference set only through a deliberate narrator action.

## Scope boundary

The analyzer may measure acoustic difference—such as pitch distribution, rate, energy, or spectral characteristics. It cannot establish whether a performance is emotionally correct, whether a narrator is "properly in character," or reliably infer fictional speaker identity without approved context.

## Success signals

- The narrator can find a potential voice drift in a long book without reviewing every line.
- Intentional changes can be dismissed and documented, preventing recurring warnings.
