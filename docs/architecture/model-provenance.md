# Model provenance and licences

Every model, voice and data pack the app can download, where it comes from, under which licence, whether it may be used for a paid audiobook, and what was decided about it. The tables under [The artifacts](#the-artifacts) are **generated** from the three catalogs the release carries (`config/whisper-assets.json`, `config/tts-assets.json`, `config/spacy-assets.json`) by `scripts/licenses/models.mjs`, so they cannot disagree with what the app downloads; the judgements (commercial use, status, notes) are written in `scripts/licenses/model-review.json`. The general policy and the record of every candidate are in the [local dependency evaluation](../research/local-dependency-evaluation.md), and the download rules are in [first-use dependency provisioning](first-use-dependency-provisioning.md). This is engineering guidance, not legal advice.

## The rules

- **Program licence.** The project is AGPL-3.0-or-later ([ADR 0039](../adr/0039-the-project-is-licensed-agpl-3-or-later.md)), so bundling GPL-family code is allowed. That is why the frozen Story Bible sidecar may contain Piper (`piper-tts`, GPL-3.0-or-later), `phonemizer` (GPL-3.0-or-later) and eSpeak NG's data: they ship in the release, with their notices and a source offer in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` ([Third-party notices](../operations/ci-and-releases.md#third-party-notices)). There is no "separate downloaded asset" split for them.
- **Model licences are still checked per artifact.** A model is data with its own licence and its own training-data terms, and the program's licence says nothing about them. An artifact whose licence is unclear, restricted or non-commercial is **marked "unclear" here and kept out of the catalog** until each artifact is cleared: no automatic download, no listing as a supported option (owner decision Q13 (b), applying the policy of the local dependency evaluation).
- **One row per artifact.** A new catalog entry needs its review row, or `pnpm check` fails (`scripts/licenses/models.test.mjs`); a review row for an artifact that left the catalog fails the same way. Changing a pin (a new revision, a new hash) changes the generated table in the same pull request.
- **Nothing downloads by itself.** Every artifact below is fetched only after the narrator confirms, checked against the size and SHA-256 recorded here, and kept in the per-user cache ([threat model](threat-model.md#1-first-use-downloads-securitymd-bullet-2)).

## Changing this document

Add or change a catalog entry, then run `node scripts/licenses/models.mjs` to rewrite the tables (`--check` fails when they are stale). For a new artifact add its row to `scripts/licenses/model-review.json` first, after reading its model card and licence at the pinned revision: state the commercial-use finding in plain words and say `unclear` when the card does not. An `unclear` artifact stays out of the catalog and appears under "Not in the catalogs". The allow-list for dependencies is separate ([dependency review](../operations/github-workflow.md)); it covers packages, not models.
