# 0141. Per-take divergence is localized by the markers' diff and ASR word timestamps, checked on synthetic fixtures

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** 2026-09-23

## Context

Take Intelligence (`docs/prds/take-review-pickups-duplicates-take-intelligence.prd.md`, phase 9) has to show where inside each take of one manuscript span the read goes wrong, so that phase 10's comparison view can shade the divergent sub-span of every take. PRD Q10 asked whether the existing approach, a free Whisper transcript with word timestamps diffed against the manuscript ([ADR 0008](0008-timing-confidence-over-forced-alignment-model-for-transcript-compare.md)), is precise enough, or whether forced alignment (a PyTorch or Kaldi dependency, a new ADR superseding 0008) is needed. The owner answered on 2026-09-23: keep ADR 0008's approach, evaluate it on synthetic, constructed fixtures instead of an owner-annotated corpus, say that this is weaker evidence than real annotated chapters, and consider forced alignment only if the fixtures show word timing is too coarse.

## Decision

- **Mode.** `compare.py --take-divergence` (`sidecars/transcript-compare/core/take_divergence_mode.py`) reads a JSON manifest: a chapter id, a span as the chapter's sentence units `firstUnit`..`lastUnit` (numbered as `--find-repeats` numbers a repeated span, so a phase 4 group's span can be passed straight in), and the takes as `{itemGuid, takeGuid, sourceFile, startOffset, length}`. Each take's range is decoded and transcribed with word timestamps, as every other mode does. The manifest is checked field by field before anything is decoded. The results file is written atomically. Exit codes are 0 done, 1 failed and 2 cancelled, as in the other modes.
- **Alignment.** Each take is aligned to the span's words with the take markers' own diff (`diff_and_build_markers`), so the same tokenizing, number merging, homophones and project equivalences apply. When a take says the same words twice (a restart or a stutter) and the diff matched the first copy, the extra is moved to the first copy. A narrator abandons the earlier read, so the extra is timed at the false start.
- **Output.** `TAKE_DIVERGENCE|{json}` per take: every span word's status (`matched`, `misread`, `skipped` between words that were read, `unread` because the take starts late or stops early) with its time, and every divergence as span words plus source seconds, marked `before`, `within` or `after` the words the take read. A skip's time is the pause where the words were left out. Unread words have no time. `fidelity` is matched words over span words. Nothing ranks the takes or turns fidelity into a verdict (PRD Q9).
- **Q10 is answered by evidence: (A) stays.** On the fixture set (`sidecars/transcript-compare/tests/fixtures/divergence/cases.json`: 14 constructed takes over public-domain text, 14 known divergences), rendered to speech with each word's true onset and transcribed with the app's default Whisper model (`small`), every divergence was reported over the right span words, with both time boundaries within 0.23 s of the truth (median 0.10 s). That is finer than one word at 150 words a minute (0.4 s), the smallest sub-span the comparison view shades. So word timing is not too coarse, and forced alignment is not adopted. Timing error was never the failure seen. The failures were ASR text errors: an invented name misspelled (the per-project vocabulary hints and equivalence list already exist for that), and with the smaller `base` and `tiny` models some misheard words. Forced alignment would not fix either. The full results and the protocol are in `docs/research/take-divergence-evaluation.md`.
- **The evidence is synthetic, and that is recorded.** A synthetic voice reads cleanly, at an even pace, with no room noise, breaths or mouth noise. A real session will time words worse than these fixtures do. The fixtures stay in the repository as a CI gate (`tests/test_divergence_harness.py`, exact and jittered timing). The real-ASR run is manual (`divergence_tts.ps1` plus `divergence_harness.py --asr`) and should be repeated on real annotated chapters if any become available.

## Consequences

- Phase 10 gets per-word statuses and divergence time ranges it can shade without re-aligning anything, and uses `fidelity` and the counts as text evidence beside the phase 8 metrics.
- No new ML dependency, no sidecar size change and no new model download.
- The mode loads the Whisper model once per take, as `--find-repeats` does. A later change can share one model across takes or reuse words files as the recording-coverage mode does.
- An abandoned read is assumed to come first. A narrator who deliberately reads a phrase twice, the second time as the keeper, gets the extra on the earlier copy, which is the right copy to cut.
- A misread right before the end of a take absorbs the unread words after it into one `misread` divergence, because the diff reports one replaced block. The span words are still right; only the kind is coarser.
- Adopting forced alignment later still needs a new ADR superseding ADR 0008 and this one. It needs evidence from real recordings that word timing, not ASR text, is what fails.
