# 0635. The nav rail and header take the benchmark sizes, and every header chip is one HeaderChip primitive

**Status:** Proposed (Phase 8 of the [mock fidelity PRD](../prds/mock-fidelity-primitives-and-components.prd.md), stream F-P8 on [#509](https://github.com/countrymanprime/narration-utils/issues/509); hiding the first group's heading departs from ADR 0407 item 3 on measurement, for the owner to confirm on [#510](https://github.com/countrymanprime/narration-utils/issues/510))
**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Amends:** [ADR 0407](0407-a-new-page-replaces-its-old-counterpart-in-the-same-change-and-the-navigation-is-grouped-by-production-stage.md) item 3 (every wide-rail group heading is drawn) and [ADR 0059](0059-text-colours-meet-wcag-aa-with-two-text-levels-a-non-text-token-and-derived-on-tint-text.md)'s "accent tint" behind the current nav item, which becomes the `--accent-soft` token

## Context

The owner found the merged pages don't match the approved mocks stylistically (D91 on #509), and the nav rail and header are on every page. The shell drew a 224 px rail with 41.6 px items in Barlow Condensed 16 px, a 56 px header, and the header's chip in four hand-written copies (`EngineChip` twice, `TimerChip`, the Booth's `RecorderStatus`), 28 px tall with a border. The current nav item's fill was an untokenised `color-mix` of `--accent` at 10%.

Benchmark mock 01 (`docs/research/mockups/audiobook-studio-benchmark/01-production-home.webp`, D92) was measured again for this phase at 1440 px, scanning edges and ink with sharp (good to about ±1 px):

The PRD's table gives the chip as "≈21 px"; the scan puts its edge at 22 with antialiasing on both sides, the same height as ADR 0600's pill, so it is 22.

## Decision

1. **The rail and header take the measured sizes.** `AppShell.tsx`: the wide rail is `w-54` (216 px), the header `h-13` (52 px), the brand block, the nav's padding, the group heading and the foot as measured above. The icon-only rail (768–1399 px) and the drawer are not drawn by any benchmark mock and keep their widths; they inherit the item's type and radius.
2. **`NavButton` is the measured item.** 34 px, radius 7, 13 px Barlow Condensed 600 tracked 0.11 em, a 12 px icon, and the current page on `--accent-soft` (the `accent-strong-on-soft` pair `paletteContrast.test.ts` already checks), replacing the `color-mix`. It gains a `count` slot: a 16 px pill at the item's end, part of the item's accessible name ("Proof, 14"), drawn only for a count above zero. Nothing passes one yet: a count is drawn when a page's count has data behind it (visual audit SH5).
3. **The first group's heading is not drawn.** Mock 01 heads Prep, Record, Review and Finish, but not the Production group, whose one item already says "Production". The heading stays in the DOM, visually hidden, so it still names the group through `aria-labelledby`, and the navigation's role tree is unchanged. This amends ADR 0407 item 3's "on the wide rail the group names are text labels" for the first group only.
4. **One header chip.** `primitives/HeaderChip.tsx` draws every chip in the header and the Booth's status line: `tone` (`neutral`, `success`, `warning`), a `dot` colour drawn with ADR 0600's `Dot`, `onClick` to make it a button (the engine chip opens the engine panel), `role` (`group` or `timer`) with an `aria-label`, and `short` for a chip whose text hides below `md`. `EngineChip`, `TimerChip` and `booth/BuiltinRecorder`'s `RecorderStatus` draw with it. The engine chip is `success` when linked, `warning` when REAPER has another project open (it was a neutral chip with a warn dot), and neutral with a `--non-text` dot when nothing is linked; the dots lose their glow.
5. **The guard.** The header chips' allowances in `src/badgeShapes.test.ts` are deleted, so a pasted chip or dot in those files fails it again.

## Consequences

- Every page's shell is 8 px narrower in the rail and 4 px shorter in the header, so each page's content area grows by that much; the visual suite captures every page again.
- The dark sets drawn from the old app (`app-navigation-and-zoom-controls/*`, `app-shell-vertical-overflow/01-after`) draw the old 224 px rail and 56 px header, so their pixel match falls a little: every pixel of the page body shifts. The benchmark mock wins where the two disagree (the PRD's Q1).
- The header keeps what the benchmark mocks don't draw and the app-navigation PRD added (Back, Forward and the zoom group, ADR 0407 item 4); they sit on the 52 px header unchanged.
- A new header chip is a `HeaderChip`, never a pasted class string; a new look is a new `tone`, in a change to this primitive.
- Drawing the first group's heading again, or a count with no data behind it, needs a new ADR that supersedes this one.
