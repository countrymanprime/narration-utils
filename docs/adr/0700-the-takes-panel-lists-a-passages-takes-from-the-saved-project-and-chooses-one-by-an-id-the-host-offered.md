# 0700. The Takes panel lists a passage's takes from the saved project and chooses one by an id the host offered

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-09-28

## Context

[Edit and proof workspace](../prds/edit-and-proof-workspace.prd.md) Phase 6 puts the takes of a passage on Proof's chapter view: the alternates, a comparison of them, an A/B by ear and "Use this take". The owner answered EP6 (all three sources of alternates, and a take never transcribed says "not compared" until the narrator presses Compare) and EP7 (Use this take makes the take active in REAPER, with a confirm only for the two-step add-and-activate). [ADR 0233](0233-the-app-may-make-a-take-active-by-guid-in-one-undo-step.md) already built the Lua `set_active_take`, the Go client and the DAW port role, and [ADR 0165](0165-a-take-comparison-is-one-finding-per-group-over-its-one-span-built-from-the-saved-project-and-never-ranked.md) built the take comparison for a take-review group. What was open is how a *passage* of the chapter, not a finding, finds its takes and how the page may ask the host to choose one without the page ever naming a GUID, a file or a time (threat model 5l, 4f).

Two things in the PRD did not survive contact with the code. A take-review group names its span in the sidecar's sentence numbers, and only the sidecar can number sentences (its splitter and tokenizer are the ones the markers use); the host cannot map a range of words onto them. And the comparison's manifest, which the sidecar reads, names audio, so what it may carry is a trust question of its own.

## Decision

- **The passage is whole paragraphs.** The panel offers the paragraph at the playhead; the host snaps a range of tokens out to the paragraphs they touch. The comparison manifest names that span by paragraph (`span: {firstParagraph, lastParagraph}`, an alternative to `{firstUnit, lastUnit}`; `compare.py --take-divergence` turns it into sentence units itself and says which it chose, and the host refuses an answer for other paragraphs).
- **Alternates come from the saved project, the stored alignment and the findings store, and nothing the page sends** (`apps/desktop/internal/passagetakes`). The item a passage was heard on is the one most of its words were heard on. Its candidates are: the other takes on that item (`Item.Takes`); the other retakes of its line on a fixed-lane track (`internal/retakelanes`); and the reads of a take-review group that has a read on that item. A group is matched by its read on the passage's item, not by its sentence span overlapping the passage's words as the PRD says, because the host cannot number sentences. A take that cannot be heard or chosen (a missing file, a range that moved since the scan) is listed with its reason and never offered.
- **A take is chosen by an id the host offered.** `WorkspaceUseTake(chapterId, firstToken, lastToken, candidateId)` reads the alternates again and refuses an id it does not list (`not_offered`). It then does what the candidate's source says: another take of the item is made active (`set_active_take`, one undo step); another retake becomes the lane that plays (`pick_retake_lane`, one undo step); a read from another item is added as a take (`create_take`) and that take is made active, which is two undo steps and is the one choice the page confirms first (EP7). Each request goes through its DAW port role or service, so each is gated as it is from any other page (ADR 0230, ADR 0304); REAPER's refusals (stale, recording, an older script, not connected, experimental off) are answered as a refused outcome in the app's words and never reported as a change. If the second step of add-and-activate fails, the answer says the read was added.
- **One comparison job.** `WorkspaceTakesCompareStart` starts the same take comparison job as a group's, so `TakeComparisonState` and `TakeComparisonCancel` answer for it and only one runs at a time. It saves one `take_comparison` finding whose id can be computed from the passage (`takecompare.PassageFindingID`), so the panel reads it back, and a second comparison of the passage replaces the first. Only usable takes are compared.
- **The A/B is in the panel and plays what was compared.** The panel is the approved mock `04-takes-panel-ab`: a card per take in the chapter view's right column, each with a play button that plays its raw recording, without REAPER, through take review's own player (`useRangePlayer`; no FX or edits). **One** plays one take at a time. **A/B** puts two takes in slots A and B and switches between them at the same word: the word the comparison timed in both takes, or, before a comparison, the same distance into the passage. The mock says Tab swaps; Tab moves focus, so a Swap button does it instead. Once a comparison timed the passage's words in a take, a take plays from its first to its last timed word; until then, its whole played range.

## Consequences

- The narrator can hear, compare and choose a passage's take without leaving the chapter view, and the choice is one undo step (two for a read added from another item).
- `set_active_take` now has a caller. [ADR 0233](0233-the-app-may-make-a-take-active-by-guid-in-one-undo-step.md) still holds: it is the only command that calls `SetActiveTake`, and take review's own screens never set the active take.
- A take-review group whose read on the passage's item is missing is not offered for the passage, even if its span covers the words. A later ADR can restore the PRD's overlap rule if the sidecar is made to number the chapter's sentences for the host.
- The take comparison manifest gained a second span form (threat model 4f), and the comparison job has a second caller. The comparison's finding appears in Proof's notes like a group's does.
- After a choice, the project in REAPER has changed and the stored check is out of date for that item until the narrator saves it in REAPER (the workspace's "REAPER has changes; save to update", EP11).
- Owner steps stay pending ([#510](https://github.com/countrymanprime/narration-utils/issues/510)): a scripted REAPER run of the three choices, hearing the A/B on the owner's own chapter (EP18), and checking the passage comparison on real takes.
