# Sources and provenance

## Corpus

**Route taken: synthetic (fallback route).** The PRD's Phase 1 scope calls for a LibriVox solo
dramatic reading. This sandbox's egress proxy denies `librivox.org` and `archive.org` at the
CONNECT step (`403`, "gateway answered 403 to CONNECT (policy denial or upstream failure)" per
`/root/.ccr/__agentproxy/status`), which `/root/.ccr/README.md` identifies as an organization
policy denial to report rather than retry or work around - none of that README's documented
fixes (certificate trust, proxy misconfiguration, a client ignoring `HTTPS_PROXY`) applies to a
`403` policy denial. See `../character-continuity-acoustic-trial.md#corpus-route` for the full
account.

Per the owner's stated fallback, the trial corpus is entirely synthetic: no LibriVox recording,
no public-domain audiobook, no recording of any real person, in any form, at any point. It is
generated by `synthetic_corpus.py` from a deterministic signal model (glottal pulse train
through three resonant formant filters), parameterized per "character" (`VOICES` in that file:
fundamental frequency, formant frequencies/bandwidths, syllable/gap durations, amplitude), with
a fixed random seed (`20260927`). There is no reader, no book, no chapter, and no LibriVox
public-domain statement to record, because there is no LibriVox material involved at any step.

If this trial is re-run on a real LibriVox reading (once the sandbox can reach it, or by the
owner outside the sandbox) or on the owner's own recordings, this file's corpus section should
be replaced with:

- Source URL (the exact LibriVox catalog page and/or archive.org identifier)
- Reader name
- Book and chapter(s) used
- The LibriVox public-domain statement for that recording
- Hand-checked timestamps for each reference/candidate segment, and who checked them
